### 1. OPERATIONAL URGENCY & PROJECT CONTEXT
1.1 Project `SAM-V` is a high-priority, time-sensitive indigenous Surface-to-Air Missile production program under direct review of the Integrated Defence Staff (IDS). The batch qualification testing of 24 seeker seeker head units is slated for firing trials at ITR Chandipur in November 2026.  
1.2 During burn-in thermal vacuum chamber testing ($-40^\circ\text{C}$ to $+85^\circ\text{C}$) on 05th September 2026, 8 units of the signal processing FPGA cards experienced dielectric breakdown under pulse modulation stress. Investigation by the Failure Analysis Committee (FAC Report No. FAC-772) traced the failure to unexpected lot-level wafer passivation defects from a third-tier silicon foundry.  
1.3 In order to meet the non-negotiable November 2026 flight trial window, 40 replacement MIL-STD-883 Class B radiation-hardened FPGA microcircuits must be procured, inspected, and surface-mounted onto seeker backplanes within 21 calendar days.

---

2.1 **Selection of Source:**  
   - Supplier: M/s Semiconductor Laboratory (SCL), SAS Nagar, Mohali (Indian Strategic PSU).

2.2 **Compliance with GFR 2017 & DPP:**
   - **Rule 194 of GFR-2017 / DPM Section 5.3:** "Single Source Selection under National Strategic Imperative".
   - SCL is the sole indigenous fab facility certified by the National Space & Defence Microelectronics Directorate to supply QML-V screened microchips.
   - Import through international distributors is excluded due to export restrictions (ITAR / Wassenaar Arrangement) and severe supply chain transit delays (minimum 36 weeks lead time).
