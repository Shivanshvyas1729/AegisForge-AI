### 1. BACKGROUND & PROBLEM STATEMENT
1.1 The Hydrocracker Unit (HCU) at Guwahati Refinery operates with two 100% capacity multistage centrifugal boiler feed pumps: `11-P-101A` (Primary Operating) and `11-P-101B` (Standby).  
1.2 On 08th September 2026 at 03:45 hrs, condition monitoring telemetry recorded excessive vibration spikes on the drive-end (DE) bearing of pump `11-P-101A` exceeding 8.4 mm/s RMS (against ISO 10816-3 trip limit of 7.1 mm/s). Subsequent lube oil ferrography revealed heavy ferrous particulate contamination (severity index > 85), indicating catastrophic fatigue failure of the thrust bearing assembly and balance piston sleeve.  
1.3 Pump `11-P-101A` was immediately isolated and standby pump `11-P-101B` was put into service. However, running `11-P-101B` without an operational standby poses severe process safety risks (loss of primary containment, sudden refinery throughput reduction of 18,500 BPD, and flaring loss estimated at ₹1.45 Crores per day).

---

### 2. TECHNICAL JUSTIFICATION & SCOPE OF WORK
2.1 **Scope of Overhaul:**
   - Complete de-staging and internal inspection of 11-stage diffuser casing assembly.
   - Dynamic balancing of rotor shaft assembly per ISO 1940 Grade G1.0.
   - Replacement of Tungsten Carbide mechanical seal cartridge assembly (Plan 53B).
   - Ultrasonic non-destructive thickness testing (NDT) of the suction/discharge nozzles.
2.2 **OEM Spares Requirement:**
   - OEM: M/s Sulzer Pumps India Pvt. Ltd. (Proprietary equipment design).
   - Itemized spares list:
     - 01 Set: Rotor shaft assembly (Material: Super Duplex Stainless Steel UNS S32750)
     - 02 Sets: Dual pressurized mechanical cartridge seals (API 682 Category 2, Type A)
     - 01 Set: PEEK balance bushing and sleeve assembly

---

### 3. PROCUREMENT METHODOLOGY & CVC GUIDELINES COMPLIANCE
3.1 The required pump rotor and high-tolerance cartridge seals are proprietary to the Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM, M/s Sulzer Pumps). Interchanging non-OEM components in a 140 bar hydrogen service vessel breaches OISD-171 and PESO statutory safety clearances.  
3.2 In accordance with Section 4.3.2 of the IOCL Purchase Manual and Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) Office Order No. 23/07/07 regarding Proprietary Article Certificate (PAC) procurement:
   - Proprietary Article Certificate (PAC No. PAC/GHR/2026/041) is enclosed at **Annexure-I**.
   - Single Tender Nomination is justified under urgent operational exigency to prevent refinery shutdown.
3.3 GeM (Government e-Marketplace) non-availability certificate (GNA-2026-08992) generated as customized super duplex multistage impellers are not cataloged on GeM.
