- **Gross Refining Margin (GRM):** $9.45 / bbl (against Industry Benchmark of $8.10 / bbl).
- **Distillate Yield:** 82.4% (Highest in corporate history).

### SLIDE 4: Environmental, Social & Governance (ESG) Impact
- **CO2e Emission Avoidance:** 1.85 Million Metric Tonnes / Annum.
- **Green Hydrogen Substitution:** Displacing 18% of captive grey hydrogen consumed in the Hydrocracker.
- **Specific Energy Reduction:** Anticipated MBN drop from 56.2 to 47.8.
- **Statutory Clearances:** Environmental Clearance (EC) from MoEFCC received; Stage-2 Forest clearance not required as brownfield site.

---

### SLIDE 5: Risk Matrix & Mitigation Strategy
- **Crude Oil Price Volatility:** Hedged via dynamic Brent-Dubai crack spread futures on domestic commodity exchanges.
- **Electrolyzer Degradation Risk:** Long-term 10-year performance guarantee tied to OEM availability threshold (98.5%).
- **Project Execution Delay:** Dedicated Project Management Unit (PMU) with EPC contracts structured on incentivized early-completion bonuses.
- **Cybersecurity & Sovereign Data Risk:** 100% on-premises, air-gapped supervisory control, local AI reasoning models, and zero external telemetry transmission.
