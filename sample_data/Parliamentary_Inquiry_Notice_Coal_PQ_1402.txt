PARLIAMENT OF INDIA — LOK SABHA SECRETARIAT
STARRED QUESTION NO. 1402 FOR WRITTEN/ORAL ANSWER
MINISTRY OF COAL — SESSION WINTER 2025

Subject: Coal Reserve Assessment and Stripping Ratio Discrepancies in CIL / CMPDI Subsidiaries

HON'BLE MEMBER OF PARLIAMENT INQUIRY:
Will the Minister of COAL be pleased to state:
(a) The total verified geological and mineable coal reserves classified under UNFC-111 standards in the North Karanpura Coalfield (Block A, Seam IV) under Central Coalfields Limited (CCL);
(b) Whether the current actual stripping ratio (overburden removal to raw coal production) in opencast projects like Amrapali OCP exceeds approved Ministry benchmarks;
(c) The reasons identified by CMPDI for any significant seasonal stripping ratio spikes observed during July-August 2025;
(d) The statutory compliance status under Coal Mines Regulations (CMR) 2017 for bench slope safety and water drainage; and
(e) The steps taken by Coal India Limited (CIL) and CMPDI to deploy automated, sovereign AI systems to prevent discrepancies in high-priority parliamentary reports?

MINISTRY OF COAL ACTION REQUIRED:
CMPDI HQ Ranchi & CIL subsidiaries are directed to provide certified facts, verified reserve figures (MT), and compliant draft responses within 24 hours.
